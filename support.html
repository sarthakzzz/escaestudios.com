Email hello@escaestudios.com and we'll get back to you, usually within one working day.

Frequently asked questions

How do I install a plugin?

Download the installer for macOS and open it. It places the AU version in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and the VST3 version in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3. Then restart your music software or rescan plugins.

My DAW doesn't show the plugin

Rescan plugins in your host's preferences. In Logic Pro, open the Plug-in Manager and reset or rescan the plugin. Make sure your host supports AU or VST3 on macOS.

How does the free trial work?

Every paid plugin runs fully for 3 days so you can try it in your own projects. After that, activate a licence to keep using it.

How many computers can I use?

Each licence can be activated on up to three computers. To move a licence, deactivate it on the old machine first, or email us and we'll reset it.

Do you support Windows?

Our plugins launch on macOS first. Tell us at hello@escaestudios.com if you'd like a Windows version.

I lost my licence or download link

Email us from the address you bought with and we'll send it again.
