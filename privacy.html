Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Escae Studios ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit escaestudios.com, contact us, or buy and use our software.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name and email address when you write to us or join our mailing list.
  • Purchase and licence details needed to sell and activate our plugins, such as your email address, order number and the activation status of your licence. Payments are processed by our store and payment providers; we never see or store your full card details.
  • Activation data: when a plugin activates, it sends a licence key and a device identifier so we can limit each licence to three computers.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To deliver, activate and support the software you bought.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To send product news, only if you asked to receive it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our online store, payment processor and email provider, and only as needed for those services, or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep purchase and licence records for as long as you have a licence and as required for tax and accounting. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time.
