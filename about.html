About us

About Escae Studios

An independent audio software company that builds tools for people who make music.

Who we are

Escae Studios is a small, independent company that develops and sells audio software. We write our own signal processing, design our own interfaces and support our customers directly.

What we make

Our first product line, Plugins for Breakfast, is a set of sixteen audio effect plugins for macOS in the Audio Unit and VST3 formats. They cover the everyday jobs of music production: distortion, equalisation, compression, gating, clipping, delay, reverb, chorus, panning, filtering, tape saturation, granular effects and mastering.

Each plugin is presented as a hand-made felt scene, such as a breakfast table, a bakery or a noodle bar, where every object is a working control. The goal is serious audio quality with an interface that is quick to learn and enjoyable to use.

How we work

  • Every paid plugin can be tried free for 3 days before buying.
  • One licence covers up to three of your computers.
  • Butter Spread, our stereo widener, is free for everyone.
  • Plugins are tested in major hosts before release, and updates are free within a version.
